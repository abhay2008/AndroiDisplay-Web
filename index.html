Installation & Quickstart

1

Enable USB Debugging on Android Tablet

Go to Settings $\rightarrow$ Developer Options $\rightarrow$ USB Debugging and turn it ON. Plug your tablet into your Mac with a USB-C cable and tap "Always allow from this computer".

2

Run the One-Line Automated Installer

Open Terminal on your Mac and run the installation script:

git clone https://github.com/abhay2008/AndroiDisplay.git && cd AndroiDisplay && ./install.sh
3

Pair Moonlight & Enjoy 120 FPS

Open Moonlight on your tablet, tap 127.0.0.1, enter the 4-digit PIN in your Mac browser at https://localhost:47990, and launch the stream!
